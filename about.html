About

For the fun side of mid‑century

Good chairs are great. This site is about everything else.

Mid-century modern has a serious reputation: clean lines, famous chairs, architects in black glasses. Palm Springs has plenty of that. It also has pink bathrooms, plastic flamingos, motel signs shaped like boomerangs and aluminum Christmas trees under a spinning color wheel. That side of the era gets less respect and has a lot more fun. It’s the side we’re on.

What we’re making

Two things. A shop of gifts that lean all the way into the kitsch: swizzle sticks, terrazzo, starbursts, flamingos. And stories about where all this stuff came from, and why it’s worth keeping.

What’s real so far

This is a sample site. The products are ideas, not listings, and nothing can be bought yet. The three stories were written to show the kind of thing we’ll publish.

Every photograph here was generated with Higgsfield from a written description. None of them show real products or real places, so if you fall for that pink door, we can’t tell you where it is.
